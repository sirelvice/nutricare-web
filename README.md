# NutriCare

### Understand your nutrition. Follow your progress.

NutriCare brings nutrition tools, personal guidance and health tracking into one place. It helps you understand your measurements, explore nutrition targets and build a clearer picture of your progress over time.

[Visit NutriCare](https://nutricare-web-zeta.vercel.app/)

## A clearer view of your health journey

Everyday nutrition can feel complicated. NutriCare connects your profile, calculations and saved records so you can explore your numbers with context and return to them as your needs change.

### Understand your numbers

Explore BMI, calorie needs and macro estimates with explanations that make the results easier to understand.

### Plan with context

Generate nutrition plans using your profile and review personalised recommendations. See suggested progress adjustments and choose whether to accept or decline them.

### Follow your progress

Record weight measurements, explore trends and revisit your history. Compare saved nutrition plans to see how their targets differ over time.

### Take your information with you

Review your personal report and download a branded PDF for your own records.

## Beyond the workspace

The public website introduces NutriCare, offers learning resources and provides ways to get in touch or explore partnership opportunities. The experience carries the NutriCare identity across mobile and desktop screens.

NutriCare is a nutrition and tracking tool. Its estimates and guidance do not replace individual medical advice.
