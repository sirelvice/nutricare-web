
# BMI basics: what it is and what it isn’t

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple screening tool based on height and weight.

## What BMI can do
- Give a quick “rough range” indicator
- Help monitor population-level trends

## What BMI cannot do
- Distinguish muscle from fat
- Capture health context (diet, sleep, activity, genetics)

> NutriCare uses BMI as a starting point — not a full diagnosis.
